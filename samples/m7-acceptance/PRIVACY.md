# Privacy policy: xless M7 Acceptance

The xless M7 Acceptance app collects no data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no network
service of its own. When you tap "Allow notifications", iOS asks for your permission and, if you give it, registers the
device for push notifications with Apple; the app keeps nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

Questions: <https://github.com/fmsouza/xless/issues>.
